    Part 3 – Grammar: Present Passive

    ⏱️ 30 min 📘 p.65, Act.4

    🔍 Look at this sentence:

    The crown is made of gold.
    ↓
    SUBJECT

    The object we are talking about (the crown).

    ↓
    VERB "BE"

    Use is / are / am depending on the subject.

    ↓
    PAST PARTICIPLE

    The main verb in past participle form (made, worn, used, called).

    ↓
    EXTRA INFO

    More information: of gold, by princesses, to make dresses.

    📐 The Formula:

    SUBJECT + BE + PAST PARTICIPLE + EXTRA INFO

    🔍 Step 1: Notice the Difference

    Active Voice:

    People make these shoes in Italy.

    Someone uses cotton to make T-shirts.

    Princesses wear silk dresses.

    Passive Voice:

    These shoes are made in Italy.

    Cotton is used to make T-shirts.

    Silk dresses are worn by princesses.

    Ask: What is different? Who is the focus in each sentence?

    Explain: In the active voice, the person who does the action is important. In the passive voice, the action or the object is more important.

    📊 Step 2: More Examples

    Subject Verb (be) Past Participle Extra Information
    Itiscalledsilk.
    Theyaremade ofleather.
    Itisused tomake dresses.
    Itisused formaking dresses.
    Theyareworn byprincesses.
    The bookismade ofpaper.
    Cottonisgrownin Colombia.

    Rule 1:

    We make the Present Passive with the present tense of the verb be (am / is / are) + past participle (called, made, used, worn).

    Rule 2:

    We use the Present Passive when the action is more important than the person who does the action.

    🔧 Step 3: How to Form the Passive

    Step 1:

    The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

    Step 2:

    Add the correct form of be (am / is / are).

    Step 3:

    Add the past participle of the main verb.

    Step 4:

    If needed, add by + person at the end.

    Example:

    Active:

    People make jeans in many countries.

    Passive:

    Jeans are made in many countries.

    📝 Step 4: Complete the Rules (p.65, Act.4)

    1. We make the Present Passive with the present tense of the verb be / have and a past participle.

    2. We use the Present Passive when the action is more / less important than the person who does the action.

    ✅ 1. be
    ✅ 2. more

    Part 8 – Poster Creation

    ⏱️ 20 min

    📋 Instructions

    1. Draw a traditional outfit from Colombia (or your region).
    2. Write the history (2-3 sentences): Where does it come from? When is it worn? Why is it important?
    3. Describe it (5-6 sentences) using the Present Passive: What is it made of? Who wears it? What is it used for? What colors does it have?
    4. Draw a new outfit for your region or city (3-4 sentences): What is it made of? Who wears it? What is it used for? Why do you like it?
    5. Present your poster to another pair or to the class.

    📝 Sample Answer

    Traditional Outfit: La Pollera

    History: La Pollera comes from the Caribbean coast of Colombia. It is worn during traditional festivals like the Carnival of Barranquilla. It is important because it represents Colombian culture and history.

    Description: La Pollera is made of cotton and lace. It is worn by women. It is used for dancing and celebrations. The colors are usually white, red, and yellow. It has many patterns and flowers.

    New Outfit: The Future Caleño

    Description: Our new outfit is for the city of Cali. It is made of light cotton for the hot weather. It has a bright yellow T-shirt with a salsa design. The shorts are blue and comfortable. It is worn by young people at the Feria de Cali. We like it because it is modern and fun.

    💬 Help Phrases for Your Poster & Presentation:

    Describing Traditional Outfits:

    Describing New Outfits:

    "It is made of...", "It is worn by...", "It is used for...", "We designed it for...", "We like it because..."

    Presenting:

    "Our outfit is called...", "It is made of...", "It is worn by...", "We like it because...", "Thank you for listening!"
